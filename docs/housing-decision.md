# The rent-to-own decision

Analysis of whether to exercise the purchase option on the condo unit. Decision
is due around **September 2027** — six months before the 36th month of a term
that began March 2025.

All figures are estimates against a ₱12M total contract price. Replace them with
the actual contract numbers before acting on any of it.

## The contract as it stands

₱35,000 a month — ₱28,000 unit, ₱7,000 parking. 60% is credited to the purchase
price, 40% is rent.

| | Per month | Over 36 months |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Credited to price | ₱21,000 | ₱756,000 |
| Rent (not recoverable) | ₱14,000 | ₱504,000 |

At the end of the full term the accumulated equity is **₱756,000 — 6.3% of a
₱12M price.** At the September 2027 decision point it is ₱630,000, or 5.2%.

Neither figure is close to a deposit.

## What buying would cost

Financing need at the end of term: **₱11,244,000.**

| Route | Monthly | Total paid | Interest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pag-IBIG ₱10M @ 5.75%, 30yr | ₱58,357 | ₱21,008,623 | ₱11,008,623 |
| Pag-IBIG ₱10M @ 9.75%, 30yr | ₱85,915 | ₱30,929,559 | ₱20,929,559 |
| Bank ₱9.6M @ 7%, 20yr | ₱74,429 | ₱17,862,887 | ₱8,262,887 |
| Bank ₱9.6M @ 8%, 20yr | ₱80,298 | ₱19,271,579 | ₱9,671,579 |

Pag-IBIG raised its cap to ₱10M in 2026 and its promotional rate for loans above
₱4.9M is 5.75% — but **fixed for three years only.** After repricing, the 30-year
fixed equivalent runs to 9.75%. The honest planning number is the higher one.

Three separate problems:

1. **Cash gap at closing.** Pag-IBIG's ₱10M cap leaves ₱1,244,000 to find in cash.
   A bank at 80% LTV wants ₱2.4M down against ₱756,000 of equity — ₱1,644,000
   short. There are no savings today.
2. **Monthly cost.** The cheapest realistic mortgage is ₱58,357 against ₱35,000
   now. Add condo dues, insurance and property tax and it is comfortably ₱70,000+.
3. **Eligibility.** ₱1,131,701 across 25 accounts has been in collections for two
   to three years. Both Pag-IBIG and the banks pull CIC data. An application filed
   in this state is declined, and a decline is itself recorded.

Point 3 is decisive, and it is not close. Points 1 and 2 only matter if 3 is
solved first — and solving 3 takes roughly 12 months of clean history *after* the
last account is settled.

Settling everything by mid-2027 is achievable. Having 12 months of rehabilitated
credit *and* ₱1.6M in cash *and* ₱70,000 a month of new capacity by September 2027
is not.

## Recommendation

**Do not plan to exercise the option.** Plan the exit, and plan it now rather
than in 2027.

The paid-in equity is not necessarily lost. Under the Maceda Law (RA 6552) a
buyer who has paid **at least two years** of installments on residential real
estate is entitled to a refund of **50% of total payments made** if the contract
is cancelled, rising 5% a year after the fifth year to a 90% cap. Two years lands
in March 2027 — before the decision point.

Two things make this uncertain enough to need a lawyer, not an article:

- **Does Maceda apply at all?** It covers instalment *sales* of real estate. A
  contract drafted as a lease with an option to purchase may fall outside it. The
  60/40 split suggests the seller has drafted it exactly that way.
- **Refund of what?** If it applies, "total payments made" may mean all ₱1,260,000
  paid by March 2027, or only the ₱630,000 credited to the price. That is a
  ₱315,000 difference on the 50% refund.

**Have a Philippine real-estate lawyer read the actual contract before March
2027.** Ask specifically: is this a contract to sell or a lease-option; does RA
6552 apply; what is the refundable base; and what notice is required to cancel
without forfeiting.

## What to do instead

Rent a 4BR. Then buy in cash, or close to it, once the debt is gone.

Free capacity after all debt is cleared is roughly **₱296,989 a month.** At
₱250,000 a month set aside, a ₱2.4M deposit takes 10 months and a substantial
cash purchase becomes reachable inside a decade — with no interest paid at all.

The ₱11M of interest avoided on the Pag-IBIG route is, on its own, almost the
price of the unit.
